<project_specification>
  <project_name>Restaurant Business Website Template</project_name>

  <overview>
    A restaurant-focused website template targeting restaurants, cafes, taquerias, BBQ joints, pho shops, bakeries, food trucks, bars, and coffee shops. The template emphasizes warm, appetizing, and atmospheric design with food photography as the dominant visual language and the menu as the functional centerpiece. Built on the existing Houston Web Services Astro framework, this template will feature full-bleed hero imagery, moody warm lighting aesthetics, cuisine-specific accent colors, and beautifully formatted menu displays. The design philosophy is inviting and unpretentious, proud of the food, avoiding corporate or chain-like aesthetics. Key goals include making visitors hungry and curious, providing glimpses into the restaurant&apos;s space, lighting, vibe, and personality of the owners.
  </overview>
